The beds are watered by drip lines on a timer, not by sprinklers.

## Context

Water is scarce in late summer, and the beds differ widely in how much they need;
whatever waters them must dose each bed on its own.

## Alternatives considered

Sprinklers: rejected; they wet paths and leaves as much as roots, and one head gives every bed the same dose.
